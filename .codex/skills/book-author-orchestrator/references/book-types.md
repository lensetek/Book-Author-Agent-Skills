# Book Types

## Buku Ajar

Purpose: support structured learning in a course.

Best source: RPS, syllabus, teaching notes, lecture slides, practice cases.

Required shape:

- aligned to CPMK/sub-CPMK or learning outcomes,
- progressive chapters from basic to advanced,
- learning objectives at the start of each chapter,
- examples, cases, summaries, exercises, assignments, and assessment prompts,
- language that teaches, not only reports.

Typical chapter pattern:

1. Learning objectives.
2. Concept introduction.
3. Core explanation.
4. Examples or case study.
5. Reflection/discussion.
6. Summary.
7. Exercises/assignment.
8. References.

## Buku Referensi

Purpose: provide a broad, structured reference for a field, topic, or problem area.

Best source: multiple papers, literature review, research notes, expert topic map.

Required shape:

- concept map of the field,
- thematic chapters,
- synthesis across theories, methods, debates, and findings,
- balanced coverage of key subtopics,
- examples, cases, tables, diagrams, and research directions.

Typical chapter pattern:

1. Chapter scope.
2. Conceptual foundation.
3. Development of theory/research.
4. Comparison of approaches or findings.
5. Practical or contextual implications.
6. Open issues and future direction.
7. References.

## Monograf

Purpose: present deep scholarly treatment of one focused research theme, often based on the author's own research.

Best source: one core paper, thesis/dissertation, research report, or connected research project.

Required shape:

- narrow and explicit focus,
- clear research problem and contribution,
- state of the art,
- method or analytical approach,
- findings or core argument,
- scholarly implications and contribution.

Typical chapter pattern:

1. Research problem and urgency.
2. State of the art.
3. Conceptual or theoretical frame.
4. Method/approach.
5. Findings or analysis.
6. Discussion.
7. Contribution, limitation, and future research.
8. References.

## Selection Heuristics

- Choose **buku ajar** if the source is RPS/course material and the user needs student-facing learning support.
- Choose **buku referensi** if the user wants broad synthesis across a field or several research sources.
- Choose **monograf** if the user wants a focused scholarly book from a specific research project or paper.
- If the user is undecided, propose the best fit and show how the same source would differ across the three formats.
